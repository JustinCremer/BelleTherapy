Efrain O

Efrain is a licensed and highly qualified Massage Therapist that has been practicing for over 15 years. His focus is on emotional, and physical trauma stored in the body. He has a deep understanding of the anatomy of the human body and is an excellent resource for acute or chronic pain. His preferred massage technique is deep tissue and trigger point therapy, which is excellent for athletes or anyone experiencing muscle soreness or pain. His massages are therapeutically focused and instead of using a generic routine, he listens and feels subtle nuances that your body communicates during the session to guide his deep technical knowledge and technique, resulting in healing for injured or overworked muscles.

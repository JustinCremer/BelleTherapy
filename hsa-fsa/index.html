We accept HSA and FSA cards. Please check with your account administrator if our services are eligible for you. We can provide an itemized receipt for you including our NPI number and CPT codes for the treatment.

If you are seeking massage for chronic pain or injury, speak with your doctor. While it has been long known how affective massage is to treat most soft tissue injuries, it is now increasingly accepted by the medical community. Discuss your pain or injury with your doctor; we can provide referral form to you for your doctor to complete.
